The dashboard enables HR professionals and business stakeholders to identify attrition patterns, monitor workforce KPIs, and support data-driven employee retention strategies.

# Dashboard Features & Highlights

## Business Problem

Employee attrition has a significant impact on organizational productivity, recruitment costs, and workforce stability. HR teams often require an interactive analytical solution to identify employee segments with higher attrition risk and understand the factors influencing employee turnover.

Key questions addressed include:

- Which employee groups experience the highest attrition?
- Does salary influence employee attrition?
- Which departments require focused retention strategies?
- How does attrition vary across age groups, education, and years at the company?

---

## Goal of the Dashboard

The primary objective of this dashboard is to:

- Monitor key HR performance indicators.
- Identify workforce segments with high attrition.
- Analyze employee demographics and compensation trends.
- Support HR teams in making data-driven retention decisions.
- Provide an interactive reporting interface for workforce analysis.

# Key Business Insights

From the analysis, the following observations were identified:

- The overall employee attrition rate is approximately **16%**, indicating that nearly one in six employees left the organization.
- Employees aged **26–35 years** represent the highest attrition segment.
- The **$0–5K monthly salary** group experiences the greatest employee turnover, suggesting compensation may influence retention.
- **Research & Development** and **Sales** departments contribute the highest number of attrition cases.
- Employees with relatively fewer years at the company exhibit higher attrition, highlighting the importance of onboarding and early-career engagement.
- Educational background analysis reveals higher attrition among employees from Life Sciences and Medical disciplines.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, organizations can consider the following actions:

- Review compensation structures for employees in lower salary bands.
- Strengthen onboarding and employee engagement initiatives during the early years of employment.
- Develop targeted retention programs for Research & Development and Sales teams.
- Invest in career development opportunities for employees in the 26–35 age group.
- Continuously monitor workforce KPIs through interactive dashboards to support proactive HR decision-making.
